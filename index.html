FEATURED SAAS CASE STUDY

Genesis — AI Sales & Inventory Optimization System

Repository

Problem & Solution Overview

Retailers and small businesses often struggle with overstocking, manual inventory reconciliation, and delayed financial reporting. Genesis addresses this by providing an all-in-one SaaS management system that automates stock tracking, aggregates sales data, and projects future inventory needs using predictive intelligence.

My Role: Lead UI/UX & Frontend Developer. Responsible for designing the prototype in Figma, building the dark high-contrast dashboard using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, and writing JavaScript logic for the user interface.

Key Platform Modules & Features

1. Inventory Optimization

Real-time stock level monitoring, SKU tracking, and automated restock alerts.

2. Sales Analytics

Visual graphs showing daily, weekly, and monthly revenue performance.

3. Finance Tracking

Expense breakdown, net profit margin calculation, and transaction log tracking.

4. Customer Management

Client purchase history, loyalty metrics, and customer contact records.

5. Automated Reports

One-click PDF/CSV export engine for store audits and quarterly reports.

6. Predictive Analytics

AI demand forecasting suggesting order quantities before inventory runs out.
